How to play & setup

Quick Start

  • Hover over any card → full-screen zoom.
  • Deck Editor: add cards (max 30), save for each player.
  • Battle: click DECK to draw, phase button to advance, attack during battle phase.
  • Energy is shown as cards behind dinos. Attach energy by clicking +E.

📖 Game Rules & Setup

🎲 Before You Duel – Setup

  1. Build your deck – Go to Deck Editor. Choose Player 1 or Player 2. Click any card from the pool to add it to your deck (max 30 cards). Save each deck separately.
  2. Load the decks – In Battle Arena, select the saved deck for each player, then press START MATCH.
  3. Initial placement – The game automatically gives each player a Starter Dino (Stage 1) in the leftmost zone. Each player draws 6 cards and starts with 1 energy in the bank.

🎯 Goal

Be the first to earn 6 points. Earn a point by destroying an opponent's Dino or attacking directly when they have no Dinos.

🃏 Card Types

  • Dino – Your creatures. Each has Power (attack), HP (health), Stage (1-3), and Type. Special abilities (e.g., Bleed, Poison) are written on the card image.
  • Move – Played during battle phase for extra effects (e.g., Lightning Attack paralyzes).
  • Research – Played during main phase. Examples: Dig site (search deck for a Dino), Palaeontology (search for a Move), Abandoned laboratory (stores energy to revive from GY).
  • Energy – Represented as small cards stacked behind a Dino. Each energy can be discarded to boost attack (+1000) or used to defend (once per attack).

⚡ Turn Structure (Step by Step)

  1. Start of turn – Click your DECK stack to draw 1 card (once per turn).
  2. Main Phase – You may (in any order):
    • Play Dinos from hand into empty Dino zones (only 1 Dino per turn).
    • Play Research cards (instant effects happen immediately, field cards stay).
    • Add energy from your bank to your Dinos (click +E on a Dino). You get one free energy per turn from the Energy Zone (click the +1 button).
  3. Battle Phase – Click the phase button to enter Battle Phase. Then:
    • Click ATTACK on one of your Dinos.
    • Opponent's Dinos will glow: Green = can defend (has energy), Red = cannot defend.
    • Click the target Dino. You will be prompted to discard energies for extra damage.
    • Defender may choose to spend 1 energy to block (using their Dino's Power as defense).
    • Damage calculation: (Attacker Power × type matchup + 1000 per discarded energy) − Defender Power (if blocked). If HP ≤ 0, Dino is destroyed and attacker gets 1 point.
  4. End Phase – Click phase button again, then END TURN. Play passes to the opponent.

🔥 Type Matchups

  • Water > Fire (2x) – Water weak to Electric (0.5x)
  • Fire > Grass – Fire weak to Water
  • Grass > Ground – Grass weak to Fire
  • Ground > Electric – Ground weak to Grass
  • Electric > Water – Electric weak to Ground
  • Normal and Wind have no strengths/weaknesses.

📦 Example Play

You have T-rex (Fire, 3000 Power) attacking opponent's Triceratops (Grass, 4000 HP). Fire > Grass → 2x damage. You discard 1 energy (+1000). Attack power = (3000×2)+1000 = 7000. Opponent uses 1 energy to defend (Triceratops Power 4000). Damage = 7000−4000 = 3000. Triceratops HP becomes 1000/4000.
