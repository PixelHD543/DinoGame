📖 Game Rules

🎯 Goal

First to 6 points wins.

🎲 Setup

  1. Build decks (max 30 cards).
  2. Choose which deck for each player (AI uses its own deck).
  3. Pick a Stage 1 starter from your hand (if none, starter goes to a dino zone).
  4. Draw 6 cards. Player 1 starts.

⚡ Turn

  1. Draw 1 card (click DECK).
  2. Main Phase: play Stage 1 dinos, evolve (once per turn), play research, attach energy (click an energy card, then click a dino).
  3. If you have no dinos on field, you can move your Starter to a dino zone (click starter zone).
  4. Battle Phase: attack with dinos. Defender is prompted to spend 1 energy to block.
  5. End Phase: end turn.

🔥 Type Matchups

  • Water > Fire (2x) – Water weak to Electric (0.5x)
  • Fire > Grass – Fire weak to Water
  • Grass > Ground – Grass weak to Fire
  • Ground > Electric – Ground weak to Grass
  • Electric > Water – Electric weak to Ground
