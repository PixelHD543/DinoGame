How to play

Game Rules

🎯 Goal

First to 6 points wins. Earn a point by destroying an opponent's Dino or attacking directly when they have no Dinos.

🃏 Card Types

  • Dino – Your creatures. Stage 1 can be played directly. Stage 2/3 require evolution.
  • Move – Played during battle phase for extra effects.
  • Research – Played during main phase. Some stay on field.
  • Energy – Attach to dinos to power attacks or defend.

⚡ Turn Structure

  1. Start of turn – Draw 1 card (click DECK).
  2. Main Phase – Play Stage 1 dinos, evolve (once per turn), play research, attach energy (drag an energy card onto a dino).
  3. Battle Phase – Attack with dinos. Defender may spend 1 energy to block.
  4. End Phase – End turn.

🔥 Type Matchups

  • Water > Fire (2x) – Water weak to Electric (0.5x)
  • Fire > Grass – Fire weak to Water
  • Grass > Ground – Grass weak to Fire
  • Ground > Electric – Ground weak to Grass
  • Electric > Water – Electric weak to Ground

✨ Ailments

  • Poison 💜 – Lose 1d10 HP at start of turn.
  • Bleed ❤️‍🩹 – Double damage from next attack.
  • Paralyze 💛 – Cannot attack/defend next turn.
  • Submerged 💙 – Cannot attack/be attacked, auto‑surface next turn.
  • Fortified 🛡️ – Defend without spending energy.
