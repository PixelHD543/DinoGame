📖 RULES

How to play

Quick Start

  • Click any card to enlarge it → then click "Play Card" if it's in hand.
  • Research cards open a search window – pick the card you want.
  • Deck Editor: add cards (max 30), save for each player.
  • Battle: click DECK to draw, phase button to advance, attack during battle phase.

📖 Game Rules & Setup

🎲 Before You Duel – Setup

  1. Build your deck – Go to Deck Editor. Choose Player 1 or Player 2. Click any card from the pool to add it to your deck (max 30 cards). Save each deck separately.
  2. Load the decks – In Battle Arena, select the saved deck for each player, then press START MATCH.
  3. Initial placement – The game automatically gives each player a Starter Dino (Stage 1) in the leftmost zone. Each player draws 6 cards and starts with 1 energy in the bank.

🎯 Goal

Be the first to earn 6 points. Earn a point by destroying an opponent's Dino or attacking directly when they have no Dinos.

🃏 Card Types

  • Dino – Your creatures. Each has Power (attack), HP (health), Stage (1-3), and Type. Special abilities are written on the card image.
  • Move – Played during battle phase for extra effects.
  • Research – Played during main phase. Examples: Dig site (search deck for a Dino), Palaeontology (search for a Move). Non‑field research cards go to graveyard after use.
  • Energy – Stacked behind a Dino. Discard to boost attack (+1000) or defend.

⚡ Turn Structure

  1. Start of turn – Click DECK to draw 1 card.
  2. Main Phase – Play Dinos (1 per turn), Research cards, attach energy (click +E).
  3. Battle Phase – Click ATTACK on a Dino → target opponent's Dino → choose energy discard → defender may block.
  4. End Phase – Click phase button twice, then END TURN.

🔥 Type Matchups

  • Water > Fire (2x) – Water weak to Electric (0.5x)
  • Fire > Grass – Fire weak to Water
  • Grass > Ground – Grass weak to Fire
  • Ground > Electric – Ground weak to Grass
  • Electric > Water – Electric weak to Ground
  • Normal and Wind have no strengths/weaknesses.
