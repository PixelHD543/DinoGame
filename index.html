📖 Game Rules

🎯 Goal

Be the first to earn 6 points. Earn a point by destroying an opponent's Dino or attacking directly when they have no Dinos.

🎲 Setup

  1. Build decks in Deck Editor (up to 30 cards per deck, multiple decks per player).
  2. In Battle Arena, select which deck to use for each player.
  3. Choose your Starter Dino (any Stage 1 from your hand). If no Stage 1 in hand, your starter must go to a Dino zone.
  4. Draw 6 cards. Player 1 starts.

⚡ Turn Structure

  1. Start of turn – Draw 1 card (once per turn).
  2. Main Phase – Play Dinos (only Stage 1, or evolve from field), play Research cards, attach energy, swap starter (once per turn).
  3. Battle Phase – Attack with dinos. Defender may use 1 energy to block.
  4. End Phase – End turn.

🔥 Type Matchups

  • Water > Fire (2x) – Water weak to Electric (0.5x)
  • Fire > Grass – Fire weak to Water
  • Grass > Ground – Grass weak to Fire
  • Ground > Electric – Ground weak to Grass
  • Electric > Water – Electric weak to Ground

✨ Ailments

  • Poison 💜 – Lose 1d10 HP at start of turn.
  • Bleed ❤️‍🩹 – Double damage from next attack.
  • Paralyze 💛 – Cannot attack/defend next turn.
  • Submerged 💙 – Cannot attack/be attacked, auto‑surface next turn.
  • Fortified 🛡️ – Can defend without spending energy.
